Tokenized stocks on Base

Coinbase B20 tokenized stocks (8 decimals, 1 token ≈ 1 share). Deep liquidity on Aerodrome Slipstream (USDC pairs, fee 500). Prices live from DexScreener. Click a stock to trade it.

Create a DCA plan

A plan is a public, ownable on-chain record (StockPilotPlans registry). The StockPilot agent watches due plans and prepares the swap; you execute it in your wallet. No funds are ever held by the contract.

My plans

Plans owned by the connected wallet. “Due” means the interval has passed since the last execution. Executing a due plan: (1) approve USDC to the StockPilotSwapper, (2) swap USDC → stock at the live price (1% slippage guard), (3) record the execution on-chain.

Connect a wallet to see your plans.

Ad-hoc trade

Buy a stock right now (no plan). Same flow: approve → swap → record on-chain as a trade.
